Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-20 · applies to LAST SIGNAL // 199X on Android and iOS, version 1.0.0 and later.

In short: the game collects no personal data. It works offline, has no account, no ads and no analytics, and sends nothing about you anywhere. Everything the game saves stays on your device.

Who is responsible

LAST SIGNAL // 199X is made and published by Youssef Rhanem. Questions? Contact us.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number or any account. It does not use the internet, does not show advertising, and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising software from anyone else. It does not read your contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone or other apps.

What is stored on your device

  • Your settings (sound, controls, language, touch layout).
  • Your progress: story progress, coins, unlocked items, achievements, records.
  • A play-style profile (for example how often you jump or block) that lets the computer opponent adapt to you and the coach give you tips. It is made of numbers about your play, not about you.

These files live in the game's private storage on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your device settings, deletes them permanently.

Shadow codes

The game can show a "shadow code": a short line of text describing a fighter and play-style numbers, so a friend can fight a computer opponent that plays like you. The code contains no personal information. It only leaves your device if you copy it and send it yourself.

Permissions

Android: vibration only (for hit feedback, which you can switch off in OPTIONS). iOS: none.

Links

The OPTIONS screen has a button that opens this page in your web browser. Your browser's own privacy terms apply there; this website itself sets no cookies and uses no trackers.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Because the game collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete on our side. Data saved on your device is under your control as described above. If you have a question about privacy, contact us; we answer within 30 days.

Changes

If a later version adds anything that collects or shares data (for example online play), this policy will be updated before that version is released, the date above will change, and the game will ask for your consent where the law requires it.
