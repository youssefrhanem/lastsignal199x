Questions players ask

How do I learn the controls?
Main menu → HOW TO PLAY: 24 short interactive lessons. In a fight, pause → MOVE LIST shows every move of your fighter.
The buttons are too far apart / too small for my hands.
OPTIONS → TOUCH LAYOUT: drag the stick and the buttons where your thumbs rest, and change their size and opacity.
How do I press two buttons at once?
Lay your thumb between the two buttons: the game presses both (light punch + light kick, heavy punch + heavy kick).
Can I play with a controller?
Yes. Connect it before starting the game. Two controllers allow VERSUS between two players on one device.
Does the game need the internet?
No. It works fully offline and collects no data. See the privacy policy.
How do I start over?
Your progress is saved only on your device. Clearing the game's data in your device settings (or reinstalling) starts a new save. This cannot be undone.
The phone gets warm.
The game runs at 60 frames a second at most. Lowering the screen brightness and closing other apps helps; tell us your device model if it stays hot.
